# UAE NCA ECC Compliance Monitoring Strategy

## 1. Purpose

Continuous cybersecurity compliance monitoring helps DesertTech Solutions determine whether cybersecurity controls remain implemented, effective, and aligned with organizational requirements.

Monitoring is important because having a control documented does not automatically mean that the control is working.

For example:

- A password policy may exist, but users may not follow it.
- MFA may be required, but some accounts may not have MFA enabled.
- Backups may exist, but restoration may fail.
- A vendor may have passed an assessment last year, but its security posture may have changed.

Continuous monitoring helps identify these weaknesses early and provides evidence for management, audits, and corrective actions.

---

# 2. Monitoring Activities

## 1. Control Reviews

Review whether important cybersecurity controls remain implemented and effective.

**Evidence:** Control review checklist and assessment results.

## 2. Evidence Reviews

Verify that required evidence exists and is current.

**Evidence:** Evidence register and supporting documents.

## 3. Access Reviews

Review user and privileged access to confirm users have appropriate permissions.

**Evidence:** Access review report.

## 4. Vulnerability Reviews

Review vulnerability scan results and verify that vulnerabilities are being remediated.

**Evidence:** Vulnerability scan report.

## 5. Patch Compliance

Monitor whether systems are receiving security patches within defined timelines.

**Evidence:** Patch compliance report.

## 6. Security Awareness

Monitor employee completion of security awareness training.

**Evidence:** Training completion report.

## 7. Incident Metrics

Review security incidents, severity, response time, and recurring incident types.

**Evidence:** Incident metrics dashboard.

## 8. Backup Testing

Verify that backups can actually be restored successfully.

**Evidence:** Backup restoration test report.

## 9. Vendor Assessments

Review the security posture of third-party suppliers.

**Evidence:** Vendor risk assessment and security questionnaire.

## 10. Policy Reviews

Verify that security policies remain accurate, approved, and current.

**Evidence:** Approved policy and policy review record.

## 11. Risk Register Reviews

Review whether identified risks, owners, treatments, and ratings remain accurate.

**Evidence:** Updated Risk Register.

## 12. MFA Compliance

Monitor whether required accounts have MFA enabled.

**Evidence:** MFA compliance report.

## 13. Logging and Monitoring Review

Verify that security logs are being collected, retained, and monitored.

**Evidence:** SIEM dashboard and log review records.

## 14. Incident Response Testing

Test whether the organization can effectively respond to simulated security incidents.

**Evidence:** Tabletop exercise report.

## 15. Business Continuity Testing

Test whether critical business services can continue or recover following disruption.

**Evidence:** BCP/DR test report.

## 16. Corrective Action Tracking

Monitor open audit findings and corrective actions until closure.

**Evidence:** Corrective Action Plan / CAPA register.

## 17. Management Review

Senior management reviews cybersecurity performance, risks, findings, and improvement actions.

**Evidence:** Management review minutes.

# 4. Monitoring Workflow

The monitoring process follows:

Identify Requirement
↓
Request Evidence
↓
Test Control
↓
Evaluate Result
↓
Document Finding
↓
Assess Risk
↓
Assign Owner
↓
Track Corrective Action
↓
Verify Closure
↓
Update Risk Register
