# UAE NCA ECC Governance Domain Summary

## Introduction

The Governance domain establishes the leadership, policies, responsibilities, and oversight required to manage cybersecurity effectively. Strong governance ensures cybersecurity aligns with business objectives, legal requirements, and organizational risk appetite.

---

## 1. Governance Objectives

### Purpose
Establish a structured cybersecurity program aligned with organizational goals.

### Business Value
Improves accountability, risk management, and decision-making.

### DesertTech Example
Executive management approves the annual cybersecurity strategy and budget.

### Audit Evidence
- Cybersecurity Strategy
- Board Meeting Minutes
- Executive Approval Records

---

## 2. Leadership Responsibilities

### Purpose
Ensure senior management provides direction and oversight for cybersecurity.

### Business Value
Demonstrates leadership commitment and supports compliance.

### DesertTech Example
The CEO and CISO review cybersecurity KPIs every quarter.

### Audit Evidence
- Management Review Minutes
- Executive Reports
- Governance Committee Minutes

---

## 3. Information Security Policies

### Purpose
Define rules and expectations for protecting information assets.

### Business Value
Creates a consistent security culture across the organization.

### DesertTech Example
Information Security Policy approved and communicated to all employees.

### Audit Evidence
- Approved Policies
- Policy Review Records
- Employee Acknowledgements

---

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities

### Purpose
Clearly assign cybersecurity responsibilities.

### Business Value
Improves accountability and reduces confusion.

### DesertTech Example
Security responsibilities are defined for the CISO, IT Manager, HR, and employees.

### Audit Evidence
- Organization Chart
- Job Descriptions
- Responsibility Matrix (RACI)

---

## 5. Risk Management

### Purpose
Identify, assess, treat, and monitor cybersecurity risks.

### Business Value
Reduces the likelihood and impact of security incidents.

### DesertTech Example
Quarterly risk assessments are performed and tracked in the Risk Register.

### Audit Evidence
- Risk Register
- Risk Assessment Reports
- Risk Treatment Plan

---

## 6. Compliance Monitoring

### Purpose
Verify compliance with internal policies and external regulations.

### Business Value
Supports legal, regulatory, and contractual compliance.

### DesertTech Example
Internal audits are performed every six months.

### Audit Evidence
- Internal Audit Reports
- Compliance Checklists
- Audit Findings

---

## 7. Continuous Improvement

### Purpose
Improve cybersecurity through corrective actions and regular reviews.

### Business Value
Keeps the security program effective against evolving threats.

### DesertTech Example
Lessons learned from incidents are incorporated into policies and procedures.

### Audit Evidence
- Corrective Action Plan (CAPA)
- Management Review Reports
- Improvement Tracker
