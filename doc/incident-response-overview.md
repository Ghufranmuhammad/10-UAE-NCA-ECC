# Incident Response Overview

## Incident Response Lifecycle

### 1. Preparation

Develop policies, procedures, tools, and train employees before incidents occur.

### 2. Detection & Analysis

Identify security events, validate incidents, assess severity, and determine impact.

### 3. Containment

Limit the spread of the incident by isolating affected systems.

### 4. Eradication

Remove malware, eliminate vulnerabilities, and resolve the root cause.

### 5. Recovery

Restore systems, verify integrity, and return to normal business operations.

### 6. Post-Incident Review

Review the incident, document lessons learned, and implement improvements.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

## Management

- Approve incident response policies.
- Make strategic decisions.
- Communicate with stakeholders.

## IT Team

- Investigate incidents.
- Isolate affected systems.
- Restore services.
- Apply patches and security fixes.

## GRC Team

- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Document incidents.
- Track corrective actions.
- Update risk assessments and policies.

## Employees

- Report suspicious activity immediately.
- Follow security procedures.
- Participate in awareness training.
- Support incident investigations when required.
