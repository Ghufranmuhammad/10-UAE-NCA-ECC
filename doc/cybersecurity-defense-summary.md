# UAE NCA ECC – Cybersecurity Defense Domain Summary

## Introduction

The Cybersecurity Defense domain focuses on implementing technical and operational controls to protect systems, networks, applications, and information from cyber threats.

---

## 1. Identity and Access Management (IAM)

### Purpose
Ensure only authorized users can access systems and information.

### Business Value
Reduces unauthorized access and insider threats.

### DesertTech Example
All employees use Microsoft Entra ID with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

### Audit Evidence
- MFA Reports
- User Access Reviews
- Access Control Policy

---

## 2. Network Security

### Purpose
Protect internal and external networks from unauthorized access and attacks.

### Business Value
Reduces the risk of cyberattacks and service disruption.

### DesertTech Example
Firewalls, VPNs, network segmentation, and Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS).

### Audit Evidence
- Firewall Configuration
- Network Diagrams
- IDS/IPS Reports

---

## 3. Endpoint Protection

### Purpose
Protect laptops, desktops, and servers from malware and unauthorized activity.

### Business Value
Reduces endpoint compromise and ransomware risk.

### DesertTech Example
Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) installed on all corporate devices.

### Audit Evidence
- EDR Dashboard
- Antivirus Reports
- Device Inventory

---

## 4. Vulnerability Management

### Purpose
Identify and remediate vulnerabilities before they are exploited.

### Business Value
Reduces exposure to known security weaknesses.

### DesertTech Example
Monthly vulnerability scans with remediation tracking.

### Audit Evidence
- Vulnerability Scan Reports
- Patch Management Reports

---

## 5. Secure Configuration

### Purpose
Maintain secure baseline configurations for systems and devices.

### Business Value
Reduces misconfigurations and security weaknesses.

### DesertTech Example
Windows servers are configured using approved security baselines.

### Audit Evidence
- Configuration Baselines
- Change Management Records

---

## 6. Malware Protection

### Purpose
Prevent, detect, and remove malicious software.

### Business Value
Protects business operations and sensitive information.

### DesertTech Example
Microsoft Defender for Endpoint protects all endpoints.

### Audit Evidence
- Malware Detection Reports
- Antivirus Logs

---

## 7. Logging and Monitoring

### Purpose
Collect and analyze security events to detect suspicious activity.

### Business Value
Enables early detection and faster incident response.

### DesertTech Example
Security logs are collected in a SIEM and reviewed daily.

### Audit Evidence
- SIEM Dashboard
- Log Review Records
- Alert Reports

---

## 8. Email Security

### Purpose
Protect email systems from phishing, spam, and malicious attachments.

### Business Value
Reduces phishing attacks and business email compromise.

### DesertTech Example
Microsoft Defender for Office 365 with anti-phishing and anti-spam policies.

### Audit Evidence
- Email Security Reports
- Phishing Simulation Results
- Email Gateway Logs
