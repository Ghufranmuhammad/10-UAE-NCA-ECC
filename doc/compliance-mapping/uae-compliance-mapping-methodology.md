# UAE Compliance Mapping Methodology

## 1. Overview
This document defines the structured methodology used to map, evaluate, and track compliance against United Arab Emirates (UAE) regulatory standards, cybersecurity mandates (such as the UAE NCA ECC), and internal control frameworks. It establishes an end-to-end lifecycle to ensure continuous regulatory alignment, defensible auditing, and rigorous tracking of control gaps and remediations.

---

## 2. Compliance Mapping Lifecycle
The compliance lifecycle governs how regulatory mandates are ingested, implemented, tested, and tracked within the organization:

[Regulatory Requirement]
↓
[Applicability]
↓
[Control]
↓
[Control Owner]
↓
[Evidence]
↓
[Testing]
↓
[Compliance Status]
↓
[Gap]
↓
[Remediation]

### Lifecycle Stage Breakdown:
1. **Regulatory Requirement:** Ingesting statutory laws, regulatory circulars, or cybersecurity frameworks (e.g., UAE NCA ECC standards).
2. **Applicability:** Determining whether the specific requirement applies to organizational assets, data types, cloud tenants, or business units.
3. **Control:** Identifying or designing technical and administrative security controls to satisfy the requirement.
4. **Control Owner:** Assigning accountability for control implementation, operation, and maintenance to a designated internal owner.
5. **Evidence:** Collecting verifiable documentation, system configurations, and logs proving the control is operating.
6. **Testing:** Evaluating control design and operating effectiveness through sample-based audits or automated checks.
7. **Compliance Status:** Recording the operational state of the control (e.g., Compliant, Non-Compliant, Partially Compliant).
8. **Gap:** Isolating specific control deficiencies or failures where requirements are not fully met.
9. **Remediation:** Formulating, tracking, and executing corrective action plans (CAPs) to resolve identified gaps.

---

## 3. Core Definitions and Terminology

* **Requirement:** A mandatory obligation derived from a legal statute, regulatory framework, contractual agreement, or internal security policy that the organization must satisfy.
* **Applicability:** The scope-based determination of whether a specific regulatory mandate applies to an organizational system, process, or data repository based on risk exposure and data classification.
* **Control:** A safeguard, countermeasure, or administrative procedure designed to mitigate security risks, enforce compliance policies, and satisfy regulatory requirements.
* **Evidence:** Verifiable artifacts (such as system logs, configuration screenshots, policy documents, or access reviews) collected to prove that a control is operating as intended.
* **Compliance Status:** The formal evaluation result indicating how effectively a control meets its governing requirement (categorized standardly as *Compliant*, *Partially Compliant*, *Non-Compliant*, or *Not Applicable*).
* **Gap:** A recognized discrepancy or control failure between the required compliance standard and the actual operating state of the organization's controls.
* **Remediation:** The structured process of implementing corrective actions, technical patches, or policy updates to eliminate an identified compliance gap.
* **Exception:** A formal, time-bound authorization granted by management allowing a temporary deviation from a standard control requirement due to documented technical constraints or operational necessity.
* **Risk Acceptance:** A formal management decision to accept the residual risk associated with an unmitigated gap or control deficiency without taking further remediation action, backed by risk evaluation and executive sign-off.
