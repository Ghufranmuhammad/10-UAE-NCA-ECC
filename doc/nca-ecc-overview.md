# UAE National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC)

## What is NCA ECC?

The Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC) is a cybersecurity framework developed by the UAE National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA). It provides organizations with a structured set of cybersecurity controls and governance requirements to protect information assets, critical systems, and digital services.

---

## Why was it created?

The ECC was created to:

- Improve the cybersecurity posture of organizations operating in the UAE.
- Protect critical information infrastructure.
- Reduce cyber risks.
- Establish a consistent cybersecurity baseline.
- Support national cybersecurity objectives.

---

## Who uses it?

The framework is commonly used by:

- UAE government entities
- Critical infrastructure organizations
- Semi-government organizations
- Organizations supporting government services
- Private-sector organizations adopting UAE cybersecurity best practices

---

## Why it matters in the UAE

The UAE has rapidly expanded its digital economy and smart government initiatives. The ECC helps organizations protect critical services, comply with national cybersecurity expectations, and improve resilience against cyber threats.

---

## Main Objectives

- Establish cybersecurity governance
- Protect information assets
- Strengthen cyber defense capabilities
- Improve cyber resilience
- Secure third-party relationships
- Promote continual improvement

---

## DesertTech Solutions Example

DesertTech Solutions adopts the UAE NCA ECC alongside ISO 27001 to strengthen governance, improve cyber resilience, and align its cybersecurity program with UAE best practices.
