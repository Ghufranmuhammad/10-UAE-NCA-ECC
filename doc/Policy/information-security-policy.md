## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Information Security Policy is to establish the principles and requirements used by DesertTech Solutions to protect information and information systems.

The policy supports the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of organizational information and helps DesertTech manage cybersecurity, compliance, and operational risks.

---

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Temporary workers
- Third parties with authorized access
- Information systems
- Cloud services
- Company devices
- Organizational information

The policy applies to information regardless of whether it is stored on-premises, in cloud services, or with approved third parties.

---

## 4. Policy Statement

DesertTech Solutions is committed to protecting organizational information from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, destruction, and disruption.

Management shall provide appropriate resources and oversight to maintain an effective information security program.

Security requirements shall be implemented according to business needs, risk assessments, contractual obligations, and applicable regulatory requirements.

---

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

### Executive Management

- Approve the Information Security Policy.
- Provide strategic direction.
- Review significant cybersecurity risks.
- Support security improvement initiatives.

### GRC Manager

- Maintain the ISMS documentation.
- Coordinate risk assessments.
- Monitor compliance.
- Coordinate internal assessments and audits.
- Track corrective actions.

### IT and Security Teams

- Implement technical security controls.
- Monitor systems.
- Manage vulnerabilities and patches.
- Respond to security incidents.

### Employees

- Follow security policies.
- Protect organizational information.
- Complete required security awareness training.
- Report suspected security incidents.

### Third Parties

Third parties must comply with applicable contractual security requirements.

---

## 6. Information Security Requirements

DesertTech shall maintain controls appropriate to identified information security risks.

Security requirements include:

- Access control
- Data protection
- Secure configuration
- Vulnerability management
- Malware protection
- Logging and monitoring
- Incident response
- Backup and recovery
- Security awareness
- Third-party risk management

Controls shall be monitored and reviewed periodically.

---

## 7. Access Control

Access shall be granted based on business need and least privilege.

DesertTech shall:

- Require appropriate authorization before access is granted.
- Use unique user accounts.
- Implement MFA where required.
- Restrict privileged access.
- Review access periodically.
- Remove access when it is no longer required.
- Maintain appropriate access records.

---

## 8. Data Protection

DesertTech shall protect information according to its sensitivity and business value.

Confidential and personal information shall receive appropriate protection through:

- Access restrictions
- Encryption where appropriate
- Secure storage
- Secure transmission
- Data retention requirements
- Secure disposal

Employees shall not disclose confidential information to unauthorized individuals.

---

## 9. Acceptable Use

Organizational systems and devices shall be used responsibly and primarily for authorized business purposes.

Users shall not:

- Share passwords.
- Disable security controls without authorization.
- Install unauthorized software.
- Access information without authorization.
- Use organizational systems for illegal activities.
- Introduce malicious software.
- Circumvent security controls.

---

## 10. Incident Reporting

Employees and third parties shall promptly report suspected information security incidents.

Examples include:

- Phishing
- Malware
- Lost devices
- Unauthorized access
- Accidental data disclosure
- Suspicious system activity

Security incidents shall be documented, assessed, investigated, and managed according to the Incident Response process.

---

## 11. Security Awareness

Employees shall receive appropriate information security awareness training.

Training shall cover relevant topics such as:

- Phishing
- Password security
- MFA
- Data protection
- Incident reporting
- Acceptable use
- Social engineering

Training completion shall be monitored.

---

## 12. Third-Party Security

Third parties shall be assessed according to their level of risk.

Where appropriate, contracts shall establish security requirements including:

- Confidentiality
- Access control
- Data protection
- Incident notification
- Business continuity
- Security assessments
- Secure data deletion

Critical suppliers shall be periodically reviewed.

---

## 13. Business Continuity

DesertTech shall maintain appropriate business continuity and disaster recovery arrangements for critical services.

Critical systems shall have appropriate:

- Backup arrangements
- Recovery procedures
- Recovery objectives
- Testing
- Business continuity plans

Recovery capabilities shall be tested periodically.

---

## 14. Compliance

DesertTech shall identify and monitor applicable:

- Legal requirements
- Regulatory requirements
- Contractual requirements
- Internal security requirements
- Information security standards

Compliance requirements shall be documented and periodically reviewed.

---

## 15. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be formally documented and approved.

An exception request shall include:

- Requirement being excepted
- Business justification
- Risk assessment
- Compensating controls
- Exception owner
- Expiration/review date
- Approval

Exceptions shall not be considered permanent unless formally reassessed and approved.

---

## 16. Monitoring & Enforcement

DesertTech shall monitor compliance with this policy through activities such as:

- Control reviews
- Access reviews
- Vulnerability assessments
- Security monitoring
- Internal audits
- Policy reviews
- Risk assessments
- Third-party assessments

Violations may result in corrective actions and, where appropriate, disciplinary or contractual consequences.

---

## 17. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually and whenever significant changes occur, including:

- Major organizational changes
- Significant security incidents
- Regulatory changes
- Major technology changes
- Changes in business operations
- Significant changes to the threat environment

The GRC Manager shall coordinate the review.

---

## 18. Approval

This policy requires approval from Executive Management.

Approval shall be documented and retained as evidence.
