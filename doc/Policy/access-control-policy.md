# DesertTech Solutions
# Access Control Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that access to DesertTech Solutions information systems and information is granted only to authorized individuals based on legitimate business requirements.

The policy supports least privilege, accountability, confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Temporary workers
- Third parties
- Applications
- Cloud services
- Corporate systems
- Network resources
- Privileged accounts

---

## 3. Least Privilege

Users shall receive only the minimum level of access necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities.

Access shall be:

- Business justified
- Authorized
- Appropriate to the user's role
- Reviewed periodically
- Removed when no longer required

---

## 4. User Provisioning

New user access shall be provisioned only after appropriate authorization.

The process shall include:

1. User identity verification.
2. Manager approval.
3. System owner approval where required.
4. Appropriate role assignment.
5. MFA enrollment where required.
6. Documentation of the access request.

---

## 5. Access Approval

Access requests shall be approved by the appropriate manager or system owner before access is granted.

Privileged access shall require additional authorization.

Access approvals shall be retained as evidence.

---

## 6. Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA shall be implemented for systems and accounts where required by risk, security standards, or organizational requirements.

MFA shall be prioritized for:

- Privileged accounts
- Remote access
- Administrative interfaces
- Sensitive cloud services

---

## 7. Privileged Access

Privileged access shall be restricted to authorized personnel with a legitimate business need.

DesertTech shall:

- Minimize privileged accounts.
- Use separate administrative accounts where appropriate.
- Review privileged access periodically.
- Monitor privileged activities where appropriate.
- Remove unnecessary administrative privileges.

---

## 8. Access Reviews

User access shall be reviewed periodically.

At minimum:

- Standard user access: Quarterly
- Privileged access: Quarterly
- Critical systems: Quarterly or according to risk

Reviews shall identify:

- Unnecessary access
- Excessive permissions
- Dormant accounts
- Unauthorized privileges
- Users who changed roles

Review results shall be documented.

---

## 9. Joiner / Mover / Leaver Process

### Joiner

When an employee joins DesertTech:

- Appropriate access is identified.
- Access is approved.
- Required accounts are created.
- MFA is enabled where required.

### Mover

When an employee changes role:

- Existing access is reviewed.
- Unnecessary permissions are removed.
- New permissions are approved.
- Access is aligned with the employee's new responsibilities.

### Leaver

When an employee leaves:

- Access is identified.
- Accounts are disabled promptly.
- Privileged access is revoked.
- Company devices and credentials are recovered where applicable.

---

## 10. Account Termination

Accounts shall be disabled or removed when access is no longer required.

Termination activities shall be documented.

For high-risk or involuntary termination, access removal shall be coordinated with HR and IT/security teams as appropriate.

---

## 11. Exceptions

Exceptions require:

- Business justification
- Risk assessment
- Compensating controls where appropriate
- Approval
- Defined expiration or review date

Exceptions shall be recorded and periodically reviewed.

---

## 12. Monitoring

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through:

- Quarterly access reviews
- Privileged access reviews
- MFA compliance monitoring
- Account activity monitoring
- Joiner/mover/leaver checks
- Internal audits
- Access-related incident reviews

Evidence shall be retained for audit and compliance purposes.
