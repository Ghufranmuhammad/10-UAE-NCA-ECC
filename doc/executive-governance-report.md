# Executive Cybersecurity Governance Report

## Executive Summary

DesertTech Solutions has established a solid cybersecurity governance foundation through executive leadership, documented security policies, and a structured risk management process. The organization demonstrates commitment to cybersecurity; however, improvements are required to enhance governance maturity and align with the UAE NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC).

---

## Current Cybersecurity Governance Status

Strengths include:

- Approved Information Security Policy
- Established Risk Management Process
- Multi-Factor Authentication
- Internal Audit Program
- Executive Management Support

Improvement areas include:

- Third-party governance
- Governance KPIs
- Executive dashboards
- Vendor risk management
- Continuous governance reviews

---

## Top Five Governance Risks

1. Incomplete third-party risk management.
2. Limited governance performance metrics.
3. Missing cybersecurity steering committee.
4. Inconsistent policy review schedule.
5. Limited executive visibility into cybersecurity performance.

---

## Recommended Actions

- Establish a Cybersecurity Governance Committee.
- Implement governance KPI reporting.
- Expand vendor risk management.
- Review all security policies annually.
- Perform quarterly governance assessments.

---

## Business Benefits

- Better regulatory compliance.
- Reduced cybersecurity risk.
- Improved executive decision-making.
- Stronger stakeholder confidence.
- Enhanced business resilience.

---

## 90-Day Improvement Plan

### Days 1–30

- Review governance policies.
- Establish governance committee.
- Define governance KPIs.

### Days 31–60

- Conduct governance gap assessment.
- Begin vendor governance reviews.
- Develop executive dashboard.

### Days 61–90

- Perform management review.
- Update governance documentation.
- Measure KPI performance and prepare executive reporting.
