# DesertTech Solutions – Mock UAE NCA ECC Compliance Review

## Assessment Purpose

This fictional compliance review evaluates selected cybersecurity controls at DesertTech Solutions.

The assessment uses a simple GRC testing approach:

Requirement → Evidence → Test → Result → Finding → Risk → Recommendation

---

## 1. Access Control

### Requirement
Access to systems and information should be restricted to authorized users.

### Evidence
Quarterly User Access Review Report.

### Test
A sample of employee and privileged accounts was reviewed against approved access requirements.

### Result
**Effective**

### Finding
No significant exception identified.

### Risk
Low.

### Recommendation
Continue quarterly access reviews.

---

## 2. Multi-Factor Authentication

### Requirement
MFA should protect accounts where required, particularly privileged and remote access.

### Evidence
MFA Configuration Report.

### Test
Privileged accounts were sampled and checked for MFA enrollment.

### Result
**Effective**

### Finding
No significant exception identified.

### Risk
Low.

### Recommendation
Continue monthly MFA compliance monitoring.

---

## 3. Vulnerability Management

### Requirement
Security vulnerabilities should be identified, assessed, and remediated within defined timelines.

### Evidence
Monthly Vulnerability Scan Report.

### Test
Open critical and high vulnerabilities were compared against remediation targets.

### Result
**Partially Effective**

### Finding
Several critical vulnerabilities exceeded the organization's remediation target.

### Risk
High.

### Recommendation
Prioritize critical vulnerabilities and implement escalation for overdue remediation.

---

## 4. Patch Management

### Requirement
Security patches should be applied within defined timelines.

### Evidence
Patch Compliance Report.

### Test
A sample of critical servers was compared against the latest approved patch baseline.

### Result
**Partially Effective**

### Finding
Several servers were overdue for security patches.

### Risk
High.

### Recommendation
Establish automated patch monitoring and escalation for overdue patches.

---

## 5. Logging

### Requirement
Relevant security events should be logged and monitored.

### Evidence
SIEM Dashboard and Log Review Records.

### Test
Critical systems were checked for log collection and monitoring.

### Result
**Effective**

### Finding
No significant exception identified.

### Risk
Low.

### Recommendation
Continue regular SIEM monitoring and review detection rules.

---

## 6. Incident Response

### Requirement
The organization should maintain the capability to respond to cybersecurity incidents.

### Evidence
Incident Response Plan and tabletop exercise report.

### Test
Reviewed the Incident Response Plan and evidence of an incident response exercise.

### Result
**Effective**

### Finding
No significant exception identified.

### Risk
Low.

### Recommendation
Continue annual exercises and document lessons learned.

---

## 7. Backup

### Requirement
Critical information should be backed up and recoverable.

### Evidence
Backup Logs and Restoration Test Report.

### Test
Reviewed backup completion and restoration testing for critical systems.

### Result
**Partially Effective**

### Finding
One critical system did not have recent restoration test evidence.

### Risk
High.

### Recommendation
Perform quarterly restore testing for all critical systems.

---

## 8. Business Continuity

### Requirement
Critical business services should have continuity and recovery arrangements.

### Evidence
Business Continuity Plan and BCP Test Report.

### Test
Reviewed the latest BCP test and compared tested services with the critical service inventory.

### Result
**Partially Effective**

### Finding
The latest exercise did not cover all critical services.

### Risk
Medium.

### Recommendation
Expand future BCP exercises to include all critical services.

---

## 9. Security Awareness

### Requirement
Employees should receive appropriate cybersecurity awareness training.

### Evidence
Security Awareness Training Completion Report.

### Test
Training records were reviewed to determine completion rates.

### Result
**Effective**

### Finding
No significant exception identified.

### Risk
Low.

### Recommendation
Continue annual awareness training and periodic phishing simulations.

---

## 10. Third-Party Risk

### Requirement
Cybersecurity risks associated with third parties should be identified and managed.

### Evidence
Vendor Risk Register and Vendor Assessments.

### Test
Critical vendors were reviewed for current security assessments.

### Result
**Partially Effective**

### Finding
One Critical vendor assessment was overdue.

### Risk
High.

### Recommendation
Complete the overdue assessment immediately and implement automated vendor review reminders.

---

# Overall Review Conclusion

DesertTech Solutions has established a reasonable cybersecurity control environment. Several controls were effective, but weaknesses were identified in vulnerability remediation, patch compliance, backup testing, business continuity testing, and third-party monitoring.

The highest-priority issues should be addressed through formal corrective actions and tracked to closure by the GRC team.
