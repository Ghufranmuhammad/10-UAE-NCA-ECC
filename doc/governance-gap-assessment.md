# DesertTech Solutions Governance Gap Assessment

## Current Governance Maturity

Current maturity is **Moderate**. Core governance processes exist, but several areas require improvement before reaching a mature cybersecurity governance program.

---

## Missing Policies

- Third-Party Security Policy
- Cloud Security Policy
- Data Classification Policy
- Secure Development Policy

---

## Missing Committees

- Cybersecurity Steering Committee
- Third-Party Risk Committee

---

## Missing Reviews

- Quarterly vendor risk reviews
- Annual policy effectiveness review
- Executive KPI review dashboard

---

## Improvement Recommendations

1. Establish a Cybersecurity Governance Committee.
2. Develop missing security policies.
3. Introduce governance KPIs.
4. Perform quarterly governance reviews.
5. Improve executive reporting.

---

## Overall Governance Score

**72/100 (Intermediate)**

Governance is functional but requires stronger executive oversight, formal governance structures, and continuous performance monitoring.
