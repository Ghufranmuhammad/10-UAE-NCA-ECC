# UAE NCA ECC – Cybersecurity Resilience Domain Summary

## Introduction

The Cybersecurity Resilience domain ensures an organization can prepare for, respond to, recover from, and continuously improve after cybersecurity incidents while maintaining critical business operations.

---

## 1. Business Continuity Planning (BCP)

### Purpose
Ensure critical business operations continue during disruptions.

### Business Value
Minimizes operational downtime and financial loss.

### DesertTech Example
A Business Continuity Plan identifies critical business services and recovery procedures.

### Audit Evidence
- Business Continuity Plan
- Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- BCP Test Reports

---

## 2. Disaster Recovery (DR)

### Purpose
Restore IT systems and services following a major disruption.

### Business Value
Reduces downtime and restores business operations quickly.

### DesertTech Example
A Disaster Recovery Plan defines recovery procedures for servers and cloud services.

### Audit Evidence
- Disaster Recovery Plan
- Recovery Test Results
- Recovery Time Reports

---

## 3. Backup Management

### Purpose
Protect organizational data through secure and reliable backups.

### Business Value
Prevents permanent data loss after cyber incidents or system failures.

### DesertTech Example
Daily encrypted backups are stored in an off-site cloud location.

### Audit Evidence
- Backup Logs
- Restore Test Reports
- Backup Policy

---

## 4. Incident Response

### Purpose
Detect, manage, and recover from cybersecurity incidents.

### Business Value
Limits the impact of security incidents.

### DesertTech Example
The Incident Response Team follows a documented Incident Response Plan.

### Audit Evidence
- Incident Reports
- Response Logs
- Tabletop Exercise Reports

---

## 5. Crisis Management

### Purpose
Coordinate executive decision-making during major incidents.

### Business Value
Maintains business stability and stakeholder confidence.

### DesertTech Example
Executive Management activates the Crisis Management Team during a ransomware attack.

### Audit Evidence
- Crisis Management Plan
- Crisis Meeting Minutes
- Communication Records

---

## 6. Recovery Testing

### Purpose
Verify that recovery procedures work effectively.

### Business Value
Ensures systems can be restored when needed.

### DesertTech Example
Quarterly backup restoration exercises are conducted.

### Audit Evidence
- Restore Test Reports
- Disaster Recovery Exercise Reports

---

## 7. Lessons Learned

### Purpose
Identify improvements after security incidents.

### Business Value
Prevents recurrence of similar incidents.

### DesertTech Example
A post-incident review identifies gaps and updates security procedures.

### Audit Evidence
- Lessons Learned Reports
- Corrective Action Plan (CAPA)

---

## 8. Continuous Improvement

### Purpose
Continuously improve resilience capabilities.

### Business Value
Enhances organizational preparedness against evolving threats.

### DesertTech Example
Annual reviews of BCP, DR, and Incident Response procedures.

### Audit Evidence
- Management Review Minutes
- Improvement Tracker
- Updated Policies
