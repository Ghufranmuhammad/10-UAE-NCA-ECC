# DesertTech Solutions – UAE NCA ECC Assessment

## Current Maturity

DesertTech Solutions has implemented the foundational elements of an Information Security Management System (ISMS) and has begun aligning its cybersecurity program with the UAE NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC). Overall maturity is assessed as **Intermediate**.

---

## Strengths

- Information Security Policy approved.
- Risk Register maintained.
- Multi-Factor Authentication implemented.
- Regular vulnerability assessments.
- Daily backups with documented procedures.
- Incident Response Plan established.

---

## Weaknesses

- Third-party risk assessments are inconsistent.
- Disaster recovery exercises are not conducted regularly.
- Security awareness training requires more frequent phishing simulations.
- Internal cybersecurity audits need improved scheduling.

---

## Recommended Improvements

- Conduct quarterly vendor security assessments.
- Perform annual disaster recovery exercises.
- Increase phishing awareness simulations.
- Implement continuous security monitoring.
- Improve governance reporting to senior management.

---

## Overall Readiness

DesertTech Solutions demonstrates a good cybersecurity foundation but requires improvements in resilience, supplier security, and continuous monitoring to achieve a higher level of alignment with the UAE NCA ECC.
