# DesertTech Solutions – Third-Party Risk Assessment

## Assessment Overview

This assessment evaluates five fictional DesertTech Solutions suppliers based on data access, business criticality, dependency, security controls, and available security evidence.

---

# 1. DesertCloud Services

## Service
Cloud hosting and infrastructure services.

## Data Handled
Confidential business information and personal information.

## Criticality
Critical.

## Key Risks

- Cloud service outage
- Unauthorized access
- Data exposure
- Misconfiguration
- Supplier security incident

## Existing Controls

- MFA
- Encryption
- ISO 27001 certification
- Contractual incident notification
- BCP/DR evidence
- Vulnerability management evidence

## Missing Evidence

No significant evidence gap identified during the fictional assessment.

## Risk Rating

**High**

## Recommended Treatment

**Mitigate**

Maintain annual security assessment, monitor certification status, review cloud configuration, and test contingency arrangements.

# Executive Conclusion

DesertTech Solutions has established a basic third-party risk management process, but several suppliers require continued monitoring and evidence collection.

## Highest-Risk Vendor

**DesertCloud Services**

### Why is it the highest-risk vendor?

It hosts critical systems and handles confidential and personal information. A compromise or prolonged outage could directly affect DesertTech operations and customers.

### Immediate Action

Perform a detailed cloud security assessment, confirm contractual security requirements, verify BCP/DR evidence, and review access controls.

### Longer-Term Action

Implement continuous monitoring for Critical suppliers and conduct formal annual reassessments, including certification validation, incident history, vulnerability management, and resilience testing.
