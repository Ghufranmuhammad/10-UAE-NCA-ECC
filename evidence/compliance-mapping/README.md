# UAE Compliance Mapping Evidence Folder

> **NOTICE:** This documentation and all associated artifacts pertain strictly to an **Educational / Fictional Compliance Simulation**. All company names, systems, regulatory assessments, and findings are simulated for training and instructional purposes only.

---

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this evidence repository is to maintain a structured, verifiable, and transparent audit trail of all compliance artifacts, regulatory mappings, gap assessments, control testing records, and remediation tracks evaluated against United Arab Emirates cybersecurity frameworks (such as the UAE NCA ECC).

## 2. Regulatory Framework
Compliance mapping and control evaluations within this repository are structured around:
* **UAE National Electronic Security Authority / Cyber Security Council Essential Cybersecurity Controls (NCA ECC).**
* ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management Standards.
* Internal corporate security policies and data protection baselines.

## 3. Scope
The scope encompasses all simulated operational units, cloud infrastructure environments, network perimeters, data storage repositories, and third-party vendor relationships managed by the organization within the United Arab Emirates.

## 4. Evidence Methodology
The methodology for gathering and preserving compliance evidence follows a structured lifecycle:
* **Identification:** Linking specific regulatory requirements to technical and administrative controls.
* **Collection:** Gathering verifiable artifacts (e.g., system configuration exports, policy documents, access logs, and vendor assessments).
* **Validation:** Cross-referencing collected evidence against expected control requirements to verify operational effectiveness.

## 5. Testing Approach
Controls are evaluated through a risk-based testing framework comprising:
* **Walkthroughs:** Process interviews with control owners to verify understanding and administrative adherence.
* **Sample-Based Inspection:** Technical and documentary sample reviews to test operating effectiveness.
* **Status Categorization:** Designating controls as *Compliant*, *Partially Compliant*, *Non-Compliant*, or *Not Applicable*.

## 6. Limitations
* **Scope Limitations:** Testing is restricted to documented systems, sampled records, and active cloud environments within the defined simulation period.
* **Inherent Limitations:** Compliance evaluations provide reasonable, but not absolute, assurance due to the inherent constraints of control testing and sampling methodologies.
