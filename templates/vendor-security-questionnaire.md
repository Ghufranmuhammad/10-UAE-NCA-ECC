# Vendor Security Questionnaire

## Purpose

This questionnaire is designed to help DesertTech Solutions evaluate the cybersecurity posture of third-party suppliers before onboarding and during periodic security reviews.

---

## 1. Security Certifications

Does your organization maintain ISO 27001, SOC 2, or another recognized security certification or assurance report? Please provide current evidence.

## 2. Access Control

Describe how access to systems and customer information is restricted to authorized personnel.

## 3. Multi-Factor Authentication

Is Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) required for privileged and remote access?

## 4. Encryption

Is customer data encrypted both in transit and at rest?

## 5. Vulnerability Management

Describe your vulnerability management process, including scanning frequency and remediation timelines.

## 6. Penetration Testing

Do you perform regular penetration testing? If yes, provide the date and scope of the most recent test and an executive summary where available.

## 7. Incident Response

Do you maintain a documented Incident Response Plan? How frequently is it reviewed or tested?

## 8. Breach Notification

What is your contractual process and timeframe for notifying DesertTech of a security incident or data breach?

## 9. Backup

How is customer data backed up, how frequently are backups performed, and how is backup data protected?

## 10. Disaster Recovery

Do you maintain a documented Disaster Recovery Plan? When was it last tested?

## 11. Business Continuity

Do you maintain a Business Continuity Plan covering critical services provided to DesertTech?

## 12. Data Location

In which countries or regions will DesertTech data be stored or processed?

## 13. Data Retention

How long is DesertTech data retained after the service is provided or the contract ends?

## 14. Data Deletion

What process is used to securely delete or destroy DesertTech data after termination?

## 15. Subprocessors

Do you use subprocessors to provide your services? If yes, identify them and explain how their security is assessed.

## 16. Logging and Monitoring

What security events are logged and monitored, and how long are relevant logs retained?

## 17. Security Awareness

Do employees receive regular cybersecurity and security awareness training?

## 18. Privacy

Describe how your organization protects personal information and complies with applicable privacy requirements.

## 19. Risk Management

Do you maintain a formal cybersecurity risk management program? Describe the process used to identify and manage risks.

## 20. Offboarding

Describe the process for removing DesertTech access, returning information, and securely deleting customer data when the relationship ends.
